# 🛠️ 8. Using the Graphics in draw.io

For SVG graphics:

**draw.io → Insert → Image → choose your SVG**

SVG is preferable when possible because:

- 🔍 You can enlarge it without losing sharpness
- 📐 It works well in diagrams
- 🎨 It usually looks cleaner in presentations
- 🖨️ It is suitable for high-resolution export

# 🌍 Best Combination for GeoAI / Spatial Planning

For your **GIS + Remote Sensing + GeoAI + Spatial Planning + NBS** presentations:

```text
🛰️ Remote Sensing
       ↓
🗺️ GIS
       ↓
🌡️ LST
       ↓
🌳 NDVI
       ↓
🏙️ Urban Heat
       ↓
🌱 NBS
       ↓
🤖 GeoAI
       ↓
☁️ Cloud / MLOps
```

Use **Storyset / unDraw / Freepik** for the scientific and environmental illustrations, and **Iconify / Simple Icons** for the technical logos.

> 💡 **Tip:** For a professional academic slide, don't mix too many illustration styles. Pick one main style (for example, Storyset or unDraw) and keep the same visual language throughout the presentation.
