Road-sensitive species worth naming:

Amphibians:
common toad (Bufo bufo)
smooth newt (Lissotriton vulgaris)
alpine newt (Mesotriton alpestris, today usually called Ichthyosaura alpestris)
Mammals:
hedgehog (Erinaceus europaeus, 3 records): the best example for the slide, a well-known roadkill species
red squirrel, weasel, bank vole, yellow-necked mouse
Reptile: wall lizard (Podarcis muralis)
Non-native: brown rat and coypu (Myocastor coypus). They are in the list but are not a conservation concern; coypu is invasive.

Two notes for honesty:

Amphibians have 10 records but only 3 named species. The other 7 are identified only to genus or family.
These are recorded sightings, not deaths. They show species living near dangerous roads, not roadkill.
