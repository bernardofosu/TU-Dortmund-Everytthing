## 1. 🦔 Focus species and status

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 🌍 IUCN Red List | Moved from **Least Concern to Near Threatened** (28 Oct 2024) | IUCN (2024) |
| 📉 Decline in Europe | Numbers shrank in **more than half** of its range countries; **16 to 33 %** in ten years nationally | IUCN (2024) |
| 📉 Local decline | **Up to 50 %** in Bavaria (Germany) and Flanders (Belgium) | IUCN (2024) |
| 🇩🇪 German Red List | **Vorwarnliste** (early warning list), previously not threatened | Meinig et al. (2020); Rote-Liste-Zentrum (2020) |
| 📊 Trend Germany | Current status "common"; long-term **decline, extent unknown**; short-term **moderate decline** | Rote-Liste-Zentrum (2020) |
| 🚗 Roadkill trend | Bavarian roadkill counts fell to about **one fifth of the 1976 level** | Meinig et al. (2020) |
| ⚖️ Legal protection | **Specially protected** (§ 7 (2) no. 13 BNatSchG with Annex 1 BArtSchV); killing, injuring, disturbing prohibited (§ 44 (1) BNatSchG) | Stadt Münster (2025) |
| 🌍 Threat causes | Agricultural intensification, roads, urban development | IUCN (2024) |

## 2. 🔄 Life cycle and species needs

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 😴 Hibernation start | **September to December** | Crawford et al. (2025) |
| 🌱 Hibernation end | **Late February to early April** | Crawford et al. (2025) |
| ⏱️ Hibernation length | **109 to 168 days**, mean **140 days** | Crawford et al. (2025) |
| 🔁 Arousals | About **every 5.9 days**; 84 % of hibernation in torpor | Crawford et al. (2025) |
| 🍼 Births | First litters **May to August**; mating soon after hibernation | Kadlecova et al. (2023) |
| 🤰 Gestation | About **34 days** | Kadlecova et al. (2023) |
| 👶 Litter size | Usually **4 to 5** young, up to 11 | Kadlecova et al. (2023) |
| 2️⃣ Second litter | Possible when autumn resources are rich | Kadlecova et al. (2023) |
| ⚖️ Weaning weight | About **250 g** | Kadlecova et al. (2023) |
| 🗺️ Urban home range | **11 to 22 ha** (95 % kernel) | Korslund et al. (2024) |
| 🚶 Nightly distance | **0 to 667 m** tracked (urban Norway) | Korslund et al. (2024) |
| 🚶 Nightly distance | Up to **2 km per night** | Hedgehog Street (2023) |
| 🏠 Nests | **28 nests**: **16** in or under buildings (garages, porches), **12** in hedges, bushes, woodland | Korslund et al. (2024) |
| 🌲 Winter nests | Under **tree roots** in small natural patches | Korslund et al. (2024) |
| 🌳 Urban habitat | Gardens, parks, **cemeteries**, unused land | Museum für Naturkunde Berlin (2024) |
| 🍂 Hibernation sites | Leaf piles, stone piles, hedges | Museum für Naturkunde Berlin (2024) |
| 🐛 Diet | Mainly **invertebrates** (beetles, earthworms, slugs) | Kadlecova et al. (2023); Museum für Naturkunde Berlin (2024) |
| 🌙 Activity | **Nocturnal** | Museum für Naturkunde Berlin (2024) |

## 3. ⚠️ Risks and mortality

| Risk | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 🚗 Roads | Roadkill decline in Bavaria to one fifth of 1976 | Meinig et al. (2020) |
| 🚗 Roads | Roads fragment habitat and isolate populations | Stadt Münster (2025) |
| 🤖 Robotic mowers | **None of 18 models** detected juvenile hedgehogs **under 200 g** | Rasmussen et al. (2021) |
| 🤖 Robotic mowers | All models had to **touch** the hedgehog to detect it | Rasmussen et al. (2021) |
| 🤖 Robotic mowers | Injury by mowers increasingly reported in Germany | Meinig et al. (2020) |
| 🧱 Fences | Closed fences block movement; gaps needed | Hedgehog Street (2023) |
| 🏙️ Development | Building and busy traffic routes cause declines | Museum für Naturkunde Berlin (2024) |

## 4. 🌦️ Weather and climate (link to our Climate map)

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 🌡️ Hibernation temperature | Mean **9.2 °C** during hibernation (England, mild winter) | Crawford et al. (2025) |
| 🌡️ Dortmund warming | **+0.56 K per decade** (1991 to 2024, p < 0.001) | own calculation, Stadt Dortmund (2025) |
| 💧 Dortmund humidity | **-1.55 points per decade** (p < 0.001) | own calculation, Stadt Dortmund (2025) |
| 🌧️ Dortmund rain | **-57 mm per decade** (p = 0.025) | own calculation, Stadt Dortmund (2025) |
| 🥶 Winter 2025/26 | Coldest **-10.1 °C** in Westfalenpark (11 Jan 2026) | own calculation, Stadt Dortmund (2026) |
| ❄️ Frost days | **27 to 36** (Dec 2025 to Feb 2026, sensors) | own calculation, Stadt Dortmund (2026) |
| 🔥 Summer 2025 | Built-up nights **0.6 to 2.4 K** warmer than Westfalenpark | own calculation, Stadt Dortmund (2026) |

💡 Interpretation to check: warmer winters may shorten hibernation (Crawford et al. 2025 show hibernation varies by up to 93 days in start date); drier summers may reduce earthworms and slugs. ⚠️ Needs a source before it goes on the poster.

## 6. 🛠️ Ecological optimization (measures)

| Measure | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 🕳️ Hedgehog highways | **13 × 13 cm** gaps in garden fences | Hedgehog Street (2023) |
| 🍂 Shelter | Leaf, twig and wood piles; hedges | Museum für Naturkunde Berlin (2024) |
| 🌙 No night mowing | No robotic mowers at night or dusk | Stadt Münster (2025); Rasmussen et al. (2021) |
| 🚦 Safe crossings | Crossings and lower speed between habitat patches | own proposal |
| 🔗 Connect patches | Westfalenpark, Ostfriedhof, allotments into one hedgehog network | own proposal |
